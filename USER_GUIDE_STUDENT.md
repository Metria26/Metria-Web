# Metria — User Guide for Students

This guide explains everything a student can do in Metria today, step by step. It is written for everyday use and assumes no technical knowledge. It describes the current version of the app (October 2026).

---

## 1. What you can do as a student

- See your **dashboard** with your latest marks, upcoming assessments, announcements and evaluation bookings.
- Browse the courses (offerings) available in your university.
- Open your **marksheet** for a course and see your released scores, overall percentage and grade.
- Book an evaluation slot and cancel it before the cutoff.
- Read announcements posted by your teaching staff for your sections.

What you cannot do: enroll yourself in a course, change a mark, or create announcements. Those actions belong to your course staff.

---

## 2. Signing in

1. Open the Metria website and choose **Open app**.
2. Click **Continue with Google** and choose your university Google account (`@lhr.nu.edu.pk`).
3. After signing in you land on your **Dashboard**.

If you use a different email domain, you will see **Access not granted** — sign in with your university account instead.

If you can sign in but the app looks completely empty, your account may not be activated yet or you may not be enrolled in any course. Contact your course staff or the platform administrator.

---

## 3. First-visit banner (ads and cookies)

On your first visit you may see a small banner titled **"Ads and analytics on Metria"** with two choices:

- **Accept all** — allows advertising and visit counting.
- **Reject non-essential** — the app still works normally; you simply do not consent to non-essential scripts.

Advertising, where shown, is always labelled **Advertisement** or **Sponsored**. Ads never change your marks, bookings or course data.

---

## 4. Finding your way around

On a computer, the left sidebar shows:

| Menu item | What it is for |
| --- | --- |
| **Dashboard** | Your overview: marks, bookings, announcements, upcoming assessments |
| **Courses** | The list of course offerings at your university |
| **Bookings** | Your evaluation periods and slots |
| **Calendar**, **Messages**, **Profile** | Not available yet — tapping them shows "Page not found" |

On a phone, the bottom bar shows Dashboard, Bookings, Calendar, Messages and Profile. **Courses is not on the mobile bar in the current version** — use the links on your dashboard to open a course, or use a computer to browse all courses.

**Sign out** is at the bottom of the sidebar on a computer, and at the top right on a phone.

---

## 5. Your dashboard

The dashboard is the first screen after sign-in. It has four sections:

### Latest marks

Each card shows the assessment, the course, the date and your score (e.g. `8 / 10`), or **Pending** if the score has not been released yet. Click **Marksheet** to open the full marksheet for that course. If nothing is released yet you will see **"No released marks yet."**

### Bookings

Each card shows an evaluation period, the course, the date and time, the location (if set), how many seats are taken (e.g. `12/30 seats`) and **Booked** if you already booked. Click **Bookings** on the card to open that period. If none are open you will see **"No upcoming evaluation slots."**

### Announcements

Announcements from your sections appear here with the course and section name. If there are none: **"No announcements for your sections yet."**

### Upcoming assessments

Assessments released by your staff, with the type and weight (e.g. `Quiz · 10%`). Click **Open** to go to the course page.

If your dashboard is completely empty, it shows a **Browse offerings** button linking to the course list.

---

## 6. Courses

1. Go to **Courses**. You will see all offerings at your university with their course code, title, offering code and term.
2. Click an offering to open it.

On the course page you can see:

- **Sections** — the class groups and their capacity.
- **Assessments** — each assessment's type, total marks and weight.
- **Announcements** — everything posted for the sections (only announcements for your sections are visible to you).
- **View marksheet** — this button appears when you have released marks for the course.

You cannot enroll yourself here. If a course is missing from your marks or bookings, ask your course staff to enroll you in the correct section.

---

## 7. Your marksheet

Open it from **Dashboard → Marksheet** or from the course page (**View marksheet**).

The marksheet shows:

- **Overall** — your weighted percentage, with a note like "of 40% graded" (so you know how much of the course has been graded so far).
- **Grade** — your current grade and the grading scale name.
- **Class statistics** — your rank and the class mean, median and range, but only when enough students (usually at least 5) have scores. Otherwise you will see a privacy message such as *"Class statistics need 5 students with scores"*. This is normal.
- **Assessments** — one row per assessment with its weight and total marks, and either your score (e.g. `8 / 10`) or a status: **Awaiting score** (submitted/being graded) or **Not entered** (staff have not added anything yet).
- **Grading scale** — the grade bands (for example, A ≥ 85%, A- ≥ 80%, and so on).

If advertising is active on the marksheet, your marks start hidden behind a **Show marks** button. Tap it to reveal them, and **Hide marks** to hide them again. This keeps your scores out of reach of third-party ad code.

If you see **"Marksheet unavailable — You are not enrolled in this offering"**, your enrollment is missing or not active; contact your course staff.

---

## 8. Evaluation bookings

1. Go to **Bookings**. You will see the evaluation periods created for your sections. If none exist yet: **"No evaluation periods yet"**.
2. Click **View** on a period to open it.
3. The period page shows the booking window and a reminder like *"Booking changes close 6h before each slot."*
4. Each slot shows its date and time, location (if any), and availability such as **"3 of 30 seats open"**.
5. Click **Book slot** to reserve your place. The slot then shows **Booked** and a **Cancel booking** button.
6. You can hold **one booking per period**. If you already have one, other slots show *"You already have a booking in this period."*
7. If a slot has no seats left, it shows **Full** and the booking button is disabled.

### Cancelling

- Click **Cancel booking** on your booked slot.
- Cancellation closes a few hours before the slot starts (the exact number of hours is shown on the period page).
- After the cutoff, the button is disabled and explains why: **"Cancellation closed: the cutoff has passed."** or **"This slot already took place."**

The list of who booked each slot is visible to staff only — other students never see your booking.

---

## 9. Announcements

Announcements appear in two places:

- On your **Dashboard**, in the Announcements section.
- On the **course page**, in the Announcements section.

They may use simple formatting (bold, italic, bullet lists). Only announcements for sections you are enrolled in are shown to you.

## 10. Common messages and what they mean

| Message | What it means / what to do |
| --- | --- |
| **Access not granted** | You signed in with an email domain that is not allowed. Use your university Google account. |
| Dashboard is completely empty | Your account may not be activated, or you are not enrolled in any course. Contact your course staff or the platform administrator. |
| **"No released marks yet."** | Staff have not released any marks for you. |
| **"Marksheet unavailable — You are not enrolled in this offering."** | Ask your course staff to enroll you in the correct section. |
| **"No evaluation periods yet"** | Staff have not opened a booking period for your section. |
| **Full** | No seats left in that slot. Check other slots in the period. |
| **"You already have a booking in this period."** | You can hold one booking per period. Cancel your current one if you want a different slot before the cutoff. |
| **"Cancellation closed: the cutoff has passed."** | Too late to cancel this slot. |
| **"Something went wrong. Please try again."** | A temporary problem. Retry; if it continues, sign out and sign in again. |
| Session expired / signed out unexpectedly | Sign in again with Google. |

## 11. Not available yet (so you don't go hunting)

- **Calendar**, **Messages** and **Profile** pages.
- Booking waitlists, slot swaps and reminders.
- Coursework submission, messaging, reviews and push notifications.
- Editing your own enrollment — course staff manage enrollments.
